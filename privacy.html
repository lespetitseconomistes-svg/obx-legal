Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

1. Scope

This policy describes how the "Out of the Box" application (OBX) handles data when connected to a social media account such as TikTok. The application is an internal tool used by the OBX account owner to publish OBX's own content. It is not offered to the general public.

2. Data we access

When the account owner authorizes the application, we receive an access token and the minimum account information needed to publish content (for example the account identifier and display name). We do not collect data about other users, viewers, followers or visitors.

3. How we use it

These data are used only to upload and publish OBX's own content on the owner's account. We do not sell, rent, share or use them for advertising, profiling or analytics of third parties.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer, in a private file that is not published or shared. Tokens expire and can be revoked at any time from the platform's settings.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only as long as the connection is active. The owner can revoke access at any time in the platform settings; on request we delete any remaining data.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you may request access to, correction or deletion of any personal data we hold. Contact: clarrain@albertschool.com.
